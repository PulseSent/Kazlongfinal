# SEO and AI Search Review: kazlong.com homepage

This is a review only. Nothing on the site changes until you approve.

## Priority 1: High impact

1. **Make page text visible to search tools without running scripts.** Right now the site sends a blank page, and the browser fills in the text afterward. Google can usually read it, but many AI search tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, and others) cannot, so they see almost nothing. Fix: upgrade to Lovable's newer setup, which builds the page on the server, or at least add a short plain-text summary of the firm (services, area served, phone) that loads with the page.
2. **Add location details.** The site never names a city, county, or office address, only "Nebraska." Local legal searches ("DUI lawyer Omaha", "criminal defense attorney Lincoln NE") depend on location. Add the city or cities served, an office address (or "serving X County"), and keep the name, address and phone the same everywhere online.
3. **Rewrite the page title and description.**
   - Title (about 60 characters), for example: "Criminal Defense Attorney in [City], Nebraska | Kaz Long Law"
   - Description (about 150 characters): services, location, phone, with no line break in the middle (the current one has a line break).
4. **Add structured data for the firm and the attorney** (hidden information that tells Google and AI tools who you are):
   - Law firm: name, phone, email, address, area served, hours, logo, list of services.
   - Attorney: Kaz Long, job title, bar admission, awards (NACDA 2020–2024, BusinessRate 2025).
   - Review summary: only if the reviews are allowed under Google's rules for self-published reviews; otherwise point to the Google Business Profile instead.
5. **Add a sitemap and point to it from robots.txt.** There is no sitemap now. Also set a canonical address (https://www.kazlong.com/) so Google treats kazlong.com and www.kazlong.com as one site.

## Priority 2: Helpful content for clients

6. **Turn practice areas into real descriptions.** Each of the 8 areas is just a label right now. Give each one 2–4 sentences: what the charge means in Nebraska, possible penalties, and how Kaz helps. Later, each could get its own page (for example "/dui-defense") so you can rank for each service separately.
7. **Add a "What to do if you've been arrested" or FAQ section** with plain answers: Should I talk to police? How much does a lawyer cost? What happens at the first court date? Can my record be cleared (expungement)? Search tools and AI answers often use this kind of question-and-answer text.
8. **Expand Kaz's bio.** Add his law school, bar admission year, years in practice, courts he works in, and his kinds of case results (dismissals, reduced charges). This helps search engines and visitors trust him.
9. **Use more descriptive headings**, for example "Nebraska DUI & Criminal Defense Practice Areas" instead of just "Practice Areas."

## Priority 3: Technical cleanup

10. Better image descriptions (for example "Attorney Kaz Long in a Nebraska courtroom"; the logo's description currently says "Grizzly bear logo").
11. Shrink the large photo and award images so the page loads faster on phones.
12. Update the footer year to the current year and add a short attorney-advertising disclaimer if Nebraska rules require one.
13. Double-check the office hours: Sunday shows the same hours as Monday–Saturday.
14. Make the menu links real links instead of buttons so search tools can follow them.

## Off-site steps (you do these)

- Claim or update the **Google Business Profile** with the same name, address and phone, and ask clients to leave Google reviews there.
- Connect **Google Search Console** to check indexing and submit the sitemap.
- List the firm in Avvo, Justia, FindLaw and the Nebraska State Bar directory.

## What I need from you before building

- Office address, or the cities and counties Kaz serves
- Bar admission year, law school, years in practice
- The Google Business Profile link, if one exists
- Whether you want separate pages for each practice area, or everything kept on one page
